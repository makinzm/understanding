   - Why I recommend: A concrete, low-cost reference for running a personal agent on serverless infrastructure; useful for seeing how agent state and tool calls are laid out on Workers-style limits.

   - Why I recommend: Policy-driven sandboxing for AI agents is a core question for anyone deploying tool-using agents; worth comparing its containment model with container/VM-based sandboxes.
3. [Recent AI models struggled to match a human algorithmic innovation](https://epoch.ai/publications/innovationeval)
   - Why I recommend: An evaluation of whether frontier models can reproduce genuine algorithmic innovation, which tempers expectations about AI-driven research automation.
4. [Caught in the Act: Probes Effectively Detect Sabotage and Catch Unverbalized Deception](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12445)
   - Why I recommend: Activation probes for detecting sabotage and deception that the model never verbalizes are directly relevant to agent safety monitoring.
5. [Rounding in Preconditioner Space: Redesigning 4-bit AdamW Optimizer-State Quantization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12444)
   - Why I recommend: Optimizer-state memory is a major training cost; a better 4-bit quantization scheme for AdamW could be directly applicable to fine-tuning budgets.
6. [When KL Regularization Misfires in Group Policy Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12161)
   - Why I recommend: Analyzes failure modes of KL regularization in GRPO-style training, which is widely used for LLM reasoning RL.
7. [How the Wiz Red Agent caught a Fortune 500 firm’s data exposure (that other tools missed)](https://www.wiz.io/blog/red-agent-financial-services-data-exposure)
   - Why I recommend: A case study of an autonomous offensive-security agent finding a real exposure, showing how agents are being applied in cloud security.
8. [Telegram Desktop vulnerability allowed any user's file to be stolen](https://beaksec.github.io/posts/telegram-desktop-one-click-account-takeover/)
   - Why I recommend: A detailed vulnerability write-up (399 HN points) on one-click account takeover; good material on client-side attack surface.
9. [SQLite 本体にベクトル検索拡張 vec1 がやってきた](https://zenn.dev/mattn/articles/6e9996319ac099)
   - Why I recommend: A vector-search extension for SQLite makes lightweight local RAG/embedding retrieval practical without a separate vector DB.
10. [写真も動画も声もまとめて0.74Bで検索。ローカルRAG向け「EmbeddingGemma 2」](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2147318.html)
