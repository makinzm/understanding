   - Why I recommend: A concrete RL technique for training long-horizon agents from their own trajectories — directly applicable to anyone building or fine-tuning agentic systems rather than single-turn models.

   - Why I recommend: A practical inference-efficiency technique for long-context serving — the kind of KV-cache optimization that translates directly into lower latency/cost for production LLM deployments.

9. [AIエージェントの「Skills」などが標準規格化　CodexやVS Codeなど対応、Claudeは未対応](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/aiplus/article/2608/10/2000000487/) (Agent "Skills" moving toward a standard spec — Codex and VS Code support it, Claude does not yet)
