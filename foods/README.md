# Food

I like eating food from different countries and understanding their culinary traditions.

In this directory, food items are organized by country and then by type of food. The structure is as follows:

```txt
<country>/
    seasonal/
        <season>.md
    culture/
        <cultural_aspect>.md
    recipes/
        <category>/
            <recipe_name>.md
```
